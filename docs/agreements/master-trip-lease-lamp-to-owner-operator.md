# Master trip lease agreement — LAMP PLLC and Owner-Operator Carrier

> Drafted to the federal truth-in-leasing regulations at 49 CFR Part 376. Bracketed
> fields are fill-ins. **Counsel must confirm before signing** — see the checklist at
> the end.
>
> **This is a trip lease, not a term lease.** Exclusive possession attaches only for
> the duration of each individual trip identified in an executed Trip Addendum
> (Exhibit A). Between trips the Owner is released and operates under its own
> authority. Do not substitute this document for
> `equipment-lease-truckllc-to-lamp.md`, which grants exclusive possession for a
> continuous multi-month term and is incompatible with split-authority operation.

## 1. Parties and scope (376.12(a))

This Master Agreement is made between Carrier and Owner and is signed by both. It
governs every Trip Addendum executed under it. No Trip Addendum is effective unless
this Master Agreement is in force and both parties have signed the Addendum.

Owner is an independent contractor. Nothing here creates an employment relationship,
partnership, or joint venture.

## 2. Duration — per trip, not per term (376.12(b))

**The term of each lease is the individual trip.**

- A lease begins at the date and time recorded on the Equipment Receipt in the
  executed Trip Addendum, and not before.
- A lease ends at the date and time recorded on the Equipment Return Receipt in that
  same Trip Addendum.
- This Master Agreement continues until terminated under Section 12. **The Master
  Agreement itself conveys no possession and no exclusivity.** Only an executed Trip
  Addendum does, and only for its stated trip.

Between trips, Owner is free to operate the equipment under Owner's own operating
authority. Carrier asserts no claim on Owner's equipment, time, or loads outside an
executed Trip Addendum.

## 3. Exclusive possession, control, and responsibility (376.12(c)(1))

**During the term of each trip lease and only during that term**, Carrier has
exclusive possession, control, and use of the equipment, and assumes complete
responsibility for its operation, as required by 49 CFR 376.12(c)(1).

Owner shall not operate the equipment under Owner's authority, or under any third
party's authority, at any time between the Equipment Receipt and the Equipment Return
Receipt of an executed Trip Addendum.

This Section is not intended to affect whether Owner or Owner's driver is an
independent contractor or an employee, consistent with 49 CFR 376.12(c)(4).

## 4. Receipts (376.11(b))

A written receipt identifying the equipment and stating the date and time possession
transfers is required at the start of each trip lease, and a written return receipt
is required at its end. Both are contained in the Trip Addendum (Exhibit A).

**Neither party shall permit the equipment to move under Carrier's authority without
both receipts executed.** The receipts are the parties' record of which carrier held
operating control at any given moment, and each party shall retain them for not less
than **[3] years**.

## 5. Compensation (376.12(d))

For each trip, Carrier pays Owner **80% of the gross line-haul revenue** for the load
identified in the Trip Addendum, or the flat amount stated in that Addendum, whichever
the Addendum specifies.

- **Fuel surcharge: 100% to Owner**, passed through in full.
- **Accessorials: 100% to Owner**, passed through in full. This includes detention,
  layover, lumper reimbursement, tarp pay, stop-off pay, and driver-assist pay.
- The 80% applies to line-haul only. Carrier does not deduct a percentage from any
  pass-through item.
- Carrier furnishes a copy of the rated freight bill for each trip on Owner's request,
  as required by 49 CFR 376.12(g). Owner may exercise this right for any trip within
  **[__] days** of delivery.

## 6. Payment period (376.12(f))

**Carrier pays Owner within five (5) days** after Owner submits the delivery documents
necessary for Carrier to bill the shipper or broker. Required documents: signed bill of
lading, signed proof of delivery, and the executed Trip Addendum with both receipts.

49 CFR 376.12(f) permits up to fifteen (15) days. Carrier contracts to five. If Carrier
fails to pay within five days for reasons within Carrier's control, Owner's remedy is
payment plus **[1]%** of the net payable per additional day, to a maximum of the
fifteen-day federal period, after which the federal standard governs.

## 7. Identification devices and their removal (376.12(e))

- Carrier furnishes Owner with removable identification devices bearing Carrier's
  legal name and USDOT number, sufficient to satisfy 49 CFR 390.21 while the
  equipment operates under Carrier's authority.
- **Owner shall affix Carrier's identification devices at the time the Equipment
  Receipt is executed and shall remove them at the time the Equipment Return Receipt
  is executed.** Owner's own identification shall be displayed at all other times.
- Carrier bears the cost of producing the devices. Owner bears responsibility for
  their correct application and prompt removal.
- On termination of this Master Agreement, Owner returns all of Carrier's
  identification devices to Carrier within **[7] days**, and Carrier furnishes Owner a
  receipt for their return.

## 8. Electronic logging and hours of service

- Owner's driver maintains a carrier profile for each authority on the ELD and selects
  Carrier's profile for the duration of each trip lease.
- Hours of service accrue to the driver across both authorities and do not reset on a
  change of operating carrier. Owner is responsible for ensuring the driver has
  lawful available hours before accepting any Trip Addendum.
- Owner provides Carrier access to the driver's records of duty status for any period
  operated under Carrier's authority, on request.

## 9. Operating costs and charge-backs (376.12(h))

Unless a Trip Addendum states otherwise:

| Item | Borne by |
|---|---|
| Fuel for the trip | [Owner / Carrier] |
| Tolls and scales for the trip | [Owner / Carrier] |
| Base plates, apportioned registration (IRP) | Owner |
| Fuel tax reporting (IFTA) for miles under Carrier's authority | Carrier |
| Fuel tax reporting (IFTA) for all other miles | Owner |
| Tractor maintenance, tires, repairs | Owner |
| Lumper fees | [advanced by Carrier and charged back at cost] |

**Charge-backs.** Carrier may deduct from Owner's compensation only the items listed
above as Owner's, plus any item expressly identified in a Trip Addendum, and only in
the actual amount incurred. Carrier furnishes documentation supporting any charge-back
on Owner's request, consistent with 49 CFR 376.12(h).

**No flat periodic insurance deduction.** Carrier shall not deduct a recurring weekly
or monthly insurance charge from Owner. Carrier's cost of insuring the equipment
during trip leases is accounted for in the compensation percentage in Section 5.

## 10. Insurance (376.12(j))

### 10.1 Carrier maintains

For the duration of each trip lease, Carrier maintains, at Carrier's expense:

- Primary automobile liability of not less than **$1,000,000** combined single limit,
  with evidence of financial responsibility on file with FMCSA under MC-1725755;
- Motor truck cargo of not less than **$100,000**;
- Commercial general liability of not less than **$1,000,000**.

**Carrier shall ensure the equipment is covered under Carrier's policy for the
periods it operates under Carrier's authority**, whether by scheduling the unit, by a
trip-lease endorsement, or by hired-auto coverage acceptable to Carrier's insurer.
Carrier's FMCSA filing alone is not coverage.

### 10.2 Owner maintains

At Owner's expense, for the entire term of this Master Agreement:

| Coverage | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Primary automobile liability under Owner's own operating authority | $1,000,000 CSL, with Owner's own FMCSA filing active |
| Motor truck cargo under Owner's own authority | $100,000 |
| Physical damage on the tractor | stated value, **Carrier named loss payee** |
| Non-trucking liability (bobtail) | $1,000,000 |
| Occupational accident or workers' compensation | $1,000,000 accident medical, $500,000 AD&D |

### 10.3 Required endorsements on Owner's liability policy

Owner shall cause Owner's automobile liability policy to be endorsed as follows, and
shall deliver **the endorsement forms themselves**, not a certificate of insurance
alone:

1. **Carrier named as additional insured** — ISO form `CA 20 48` (Designated Insured
   for Covered Autos Liability Coverage) or the insurer's equivalent proprietary form.
2. **Primary and non-contributory** wording as respects Carrier.
3. **Waiver of subrogation** in favor of Carrier.
4. **Thirty (30) days' written notice of cancellation, non-renewal, or material
   reduction in coverage**, delivered by mail and by email directly to Carrier at
   [CARRIER NOTICE EMAIL], not to Owner's agent or broker.

### 10.4 Continuing obligations

- Owner shall notify Carrier in writing within **twenty-four (24) hours** of any
  lapse, cancellation, non-renewal, or material change to any coverage above, or to
  Owner's FMCSA operating authority or financial responsibility filing.
- Owner shall furnish renewal endorsements and certificates not less than **ten (10)
  days** before any expiration.
- Owner shall disclose to Owner's insurer that Owner operates part-time under lease to
  Carrier, and shall not misstate the allocation of miles between authorities.
  Misrepresentation of the operation is grounds for immediate termination under
  Section 12.
- **Carrier shall not tender a Trip Addendum, and Owner shall not accept one, while
  any coverage required by Section 10.2 is lapsed or expired.**

## 11. Products, equipment, or services from Carrier (376.12(i))

Owner is not required to purchase or rent any product, equipment, or service from
Carrier as a condition of entering this Agreement or of receiving any Trip Addendum.
Any such purchase is voluntary and its price shall be stated in the applicable Trip
Addendum.

## 13. Driver qualification and safety

Owner's driver operates under Carrier's authority during each trip lease. Accordingly,
Owner shall cooperate with Carrier in maintaining a complete driver qualification file
meeting 49 CFR Part 391, including application, MVR, PSP, Clearinghouse query,
previous-employer safety performance history, medical certification, and certificate
of violations. Owner's driver participates in Carrier's DOT drug and alcohol testing
program for all periods operating under Carrier's authority.

Owner shall notify Carrier within **twenty-four (24) hours** of any accident,
citation, roadside inspection, out-of-service order, positive or refused test, or
license suspension involving the driver or the equipment, whichever authority was
operative.

## 14. Non-circumvention

For **eighteen (18) months** following termination of this Master Agreement, Owner
shall not directly or indirectly solicit or accept freight from any shipper, broker,
or consignee first introduced to Owner through a Trip Addendum, except where Owner had
a documented pre-existing relationship with that party before the introduction. Owner
may request in writing that Carrier confirm whether a given party is subject to this
Section, and Carrier shall answer within **five (5) business days**.

This Section does not restrict Owner from operating under Owner's own authority
generally, from using any load board, or from working with any party Owner sources
independently.

## 15. Indemnity

Each party indemnifies the other against claims, losses, and expenses arising out of
its own operation of the equipment during the periods that party held operating
control as evidenced by the receipts in Section 4, except to the extent caused by the
other party's negligence or willful misconduct.

## 16. Default and termination

- Either party may terminate this Master Agreement on **[30] days'** written notice.
- Either party may terminate immediately on the other's material breach not cured
  within **ten (10) days'** written notice.
- Carrier may suspend tendering Trip Addenda immediately, without notice, on lapse of
  any coverage required by Section 10.2 or of Owner's operating authority.
- Termination of this Master Agreement does not affect any Trip Addendum already in
  progress. That trip completes under its own terms, and possession returns to Owner
  on the Equipment Return Receipt.
- Sections 4 (record retention), 14, 15, and 17 survive termination.

## 17. Governing law; entire agreement

Governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee, without regard to conflict-of-laws
rules. This Master Agreement together with each executed Trip Addendum is the entire
agreement between the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior
discussions. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

A copy of this Master Agreement and of the applicable Trip Addendum shall be carried
in the equipment at all times during any trip lease, consistent with 49 CFR 376.11(a).
